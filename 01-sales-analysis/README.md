**Sales Performance Analysis**

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and discover the products, regions, and customer segments contributing most to revenue and profit.

**Business Problem**

The objective of this analysis is to help the business understand its sales performance and identify areas that require improvement.

**Objectives**

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify the best-performing products
- Analyze sales by region
- Identify the most profitable products
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Identify areas for improvement

**Tools Used**

- Power BI
- MS Powerpoint

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about sales transactions, including order dates, products, customers, regions, sales, quantity, and profit.

## Data Cleaning

The following data preparation steps were carried out:

- Removed duplicate records
- Checked for missing values
- Corrected data types
- Standardized category names
- Checked for inconsistent values
- Created calculated fields where necessary

## Analysis

The analysis focused on:

- Total sales
- Total profit
- Sales by region
- Sales by product
- Monthly sales trends
- Product profitability
- Customer performance

**Dashboard**

The final dashboards were created to provide an interactive view of the company's sales performance.

**Key Findings**

-  The USA, India and China Were the Top Profit-Making Countries
- Nigeria Had Poor Profitability in 2014
-  Tables Were the Least Profitable Subcategory in Southeast Asia
- Lancaster Was the Least Profitable US City

**Recommendations**

Based on the analysis:

-Global Superstore should continue to focus on the USA, China and India markets and ensure that the best performing products are well stocked and promoted.
- Global Superstore should review its discounting strategy in Nigeria and look for ways to reduce costs. Discounts should be managed carefully so that they do not reduce profit too much.
-  Management should review the pricing, discounts, product mix and shipping costs in Lancaster before increasing sales efforts in the city.

**Conclusion**

The analysis provides insights into sales performance and highlights areas where the business can improve revenue and profitability.
